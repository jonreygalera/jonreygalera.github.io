1. Introduction

Welcome to Burnwise (“we”, “our”, or “us”). Burnwise is a calorie, exercise and diary app that works fully offline. It helps you see your BMI and daily calorie goal, log food and water, time workouts with optional GPS, follow your weight, and keep a private photo diary.

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect, store, sell or share your data.

2. What We Store, and Where

Burnwise saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Profile: gender, birth year, height, weight, activity level and goal.
  • Food: what you log, including calories and optional protein, carbs and fat, plus your saved and recent foods.
  • Exercise: duration and calories and, if you use GPS, distance, speed, climb and route.
  • Weigh-ins and water you log.
  • Diary posts: captions, moods and the photos you add.
  • App settings: such as theme and reminder times.

We have no servers and no accounts. You do not need to sign up, sign in or be online to use Burnwise. None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Location

If you turn on GPS for an exercise, Burnwise uses your device's location to measure the distance and route of your walk, run or bike ride.

If you allow it, tracking can continue while the app is closed or the screen is off, but only while an exercise timer is running. Burnwise explains this in the app before your phone asks for permission. When you finish or pause the exercise, location tracking stops.

Location is used only to calculate your exercise, is stored only on your device, and is never shared. GPS works without an internet connection.

4. Camera, Photos & Notifications

Burnwise asks for these permissions only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera: only to take a photo for a diary post.
  • Photo library: only to pick a photo for a diary post. A resized copy is saved in the app's private storage. Burnwise does not look at any other photos.
  • Notifications (optional): local reminders for meals, water and weigh-ins that you turn on. They are scheduled on your phone, and no data is sent anywhere to do this.
All permissions are optional. You can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

5. Sharing & Backups

Burnwise only sends information off your phone when you choose to:

  • Share a diary post: the app creates an image and opens your phone's share sheet. Whatever app you pick (for example Instagram or WhatsApp) receives that image under its own privacy policy.
  • Save a backup: the app creates a backup file and opens the share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive or email). The file contains your data and diary photos, so keep it somewhere safe.

We never see these images or files.

6. Analytics, Ads & Tracking

  • No analytics: we don't measure how you use the app.
  • No ads: Burnwise shows no advertising.
  • No tracking: the app includes no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No data sales: we don't sell, rent or trade any information. We don't have any to sell.

7. Your Control & Deleting Data

  • Back up: save everything, including diary photos, to one file whenever you want.
  • Restore: load a backup file on a new phone or after reinstalling.
  • Delete all data: go to Profile → Delete all data to erase everything, including diary photos and reminders.
  • Uninstall: removing the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Backup files you saved somewhere else are under your control and are not deleted by the app.

8. Children

Burnwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Health Information

Burnwise gives estimates for general wellness. It is not a medical device and not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before making big changes to your diet or exercise.
