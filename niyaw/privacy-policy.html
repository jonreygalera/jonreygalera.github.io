1. Introduction

Welcome to Niyaw (“we”, “our”, or “us”). Niyaw is a calm, personal finance tracking companion designed to help you track expenses, plan budgets, manage accounts, monitor debt payments, and reach your financial goals with feline clarity and ease.

We take financial privacy very seriously. Niyaw is built on a local-first principle: your financial numbers belong to you and remain stored locally on your device by default.

2. Local-First Architecture

Niyaw operates primarily offline. All your account balances, categories, income, expenses, transfer logs, budgets, financial goals, debt records, and subscription trackers are saved directly in an encrypted SQLite database on your device.

Local Source of Truth: You do not need an account or internet connection to use Niyaw. Your financial entries remain strictly on your device and are never sent to external servers or cloud services.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Financial & Transactional Data

When you use Niyaw, the following data is processed locally on your device:

  • Account Details: Account names, types (e.g., cash, bank, e-wallet), balances, and icons.
  • Transactions: Amount, date, notes, categories, tags, payee info, and payment mode.
  • Planning & Tools: Budgets, savings goals, loan repayment records, money rules entries, and subscription renewal schedules.
  • Receipt Images: Photos or documents you choose to attach to transactions or scan via the receipt scanner tool.

3.2 Account Profile Information

Niyaw operates as a local-first application. Any profile preferences or avatar choices you configure in the app are stored exclusively on your device inside your local database.

4. How We Use Data

We process your data strictly to deliver personal finance tracking functionality:

  • Calculate balances, net worth, cash flow, budget limits, and financial stats.
  • Process receipt image scanning using device vision / OCR tools.
  • Provide reminders for upcoming subscription renewals and debt payments.
  • Import/Export financial records (CSV format) upon your explicit request.

We NEVER sell your financial data, market your transactions to third parties, or use your private numbers for ad profiling.

5. Device Permissions

Niyaw may request specific device permissions strictly when you interact with features that require them:

  • Camera: Used only when taking a photo of a receipt or scanning barcode/receipt documents within the app.
  • Photo Library / Media Storage: Used to attach receipt photos or import/export data files to your device storage.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to deliver localized reminders for debt payments, budget alerts, and subscription renewals.
Permissions are optional and are requested only when you trigger the corresponding feature.

6. User Accounts & Offline Operation

Niyaw is 100% offline-ready and local-first:

  • Local User Account: All setup, profile information, and financial data live directly on your device.
  • No Server Sign-In Required: You do not need an account, password, or third-party sign-in to use the application.

Your data stays completely private to your physical device.

7. Data Sharing & Security

We uphold strict financial data safety standards:

  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information.
  • Local-Only Storage: Your financial records stay on your local device.
  • Zero Ad-Tracking: Niyaw contains no third-party behavioral trackers or target ad networks.

8. User Rights & Data Control

You maintain 100% control over your financial records in Niyaw:

  • Export Data: Export all transactions, accounts, categories, and financial tools to CSV format at any time.
  • Import / Restore: Easily import backup files to restore your financial history.
  • Reset All Data: Wipe all local databases and saved app preferences directly from the app settings screen.
